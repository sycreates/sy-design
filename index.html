04 / About

I'm Syril Makilan— Sy for short. A graphic designer who enjoys turning ideas into visual systems people can understand, recognize and remember.

My work spans brand identity, campaign design, print, digital media, motion and illustration.

I enjoy projects where design has a communication problem to solve—not simply something that needs to look good.

I'm interested in opportunities where I can contribute ideas, collaborate with a creative team and continue developing both my design thinking and craft.
